What you see todayand where to go from here

A short overview of the Maia demo: five things we show, how they fit the way your team already works, and a few proposed next steps.

Built around how your team works

Maia keeps your work in Git, runs dbt alongside, and lets you bring across the Matillion ETL jobs you already have. Nothing here asks you to start over.

Code you can read

Pipelines, context files and skills live in your repository. You can diff them and review them like any other code.

dbt stays

Maia runs your dbt commands as a step in the same pipeline, from your own project.

Your data stays put

Maia pushes processing down to your cloud data platform. Data is processed there, not in Matillion infrastructure.

The five things we show

Brief by design. We can go deeper on any of them.

1

Mission Control and the context engine

Hand work to Maia Team on a board, and write your standards down once so every task follows them.

What you see
  • A board where tasks are created, assigned and tracked, in list or Kanban view.
  • Context files and skills in the repo that hold your naming rules, tagging and what finished means.
  • Several small tasks cleared in one sitting.
Why it matters

Less repeating yourself, and the same standards on every change.

2

Build from plain language, then change it

Describe the pipeline you want, then ask for a change the way you would ask a colleague.

What you see
  • A pipeline built from a plain-language request, following your standards without being told again.
  • A change request applied, with a summary of what else it affects.
  • Suggested tests for the new logic.
Why it matters

Faster from request to deployed, with the rules already built in.

3

Beyond building: ask your data estate and keep it running

What happens after the pipelines exist. Depending on what matters most to you, we show one or both of these.

What you see
  • Ask. An AI assistant can check recent pipeline runs and usage, and hand a task to Maia Team, through the Maia MCP server.
  • Keep it running. Failure alerts to email or Slack, a root-cause explanation for a failed run, and a proposed fix on a branch for a person to review.
Why it matters

Fewer surprises, and quicker answers when something does break.

4

Bringing a Matillion ETL job across

Your existing jobs can come across without a rewrite from scratch. We show one representative job, and we would rather do one of yours together.

What you see
  • An export of an existing job rebuilt as Maia pipelines, with a list of anything that needs a person.
  • A comparison check between the old logic and the new pipeline.
  • The Upgrade Assistant for whole projects: it reads project structure, jobs, components, schedules and task history, and never your data or secret values.
Why it matters

Keep the knowledge your team has built, and move at your own pace. Maia can run alongside Matillion ETL while you do.

5

dbt alongside

Your dbt models stay where they are.

What you see
  • The dbt Core step inside a Maia pipeline, running a dbt command from your repository.
  • The same alerts and standards covering that step.
Why it matters

No need to remove anything that already works.

What coming across involves

Honest about the edges, so there are no surprises.

Comes across

  • Jobs, components, shared jobs and schedules
  • Task history, used to help plan the work
  • Optional upgrades such as Python 2 to Python 3

Needs a person

  • Secrets and OAuth connections are entered again
  • Queue and webhook triggers are set up through the API
  • Some components need individual attention
  • It lands in a new project with a new Git repository

The best way to size it is to pick one real job and measure it together.

Security at a glance

We will walk the Trust Center and the data flow with your security team.

Data and access

  • Pushdown: data is processed in your cloud data platform
  • Single sign-on, multi-factor authentication and role-based access control
  • Full SaaS, or Hybrid SaaS with the runner in your own cloud

AI controls

  • Account admins can switch Maia Team, or just data sampling, off
  • Secrets are never sent to AI services
  • Your data is not used to train third-party AI models

For a test, Matillion-hosted Git means no connection to your GitHub is needed. It has no pull requests or branch protection, so you would move to your own repository once that is approved.
